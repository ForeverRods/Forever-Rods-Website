What does a custom rod cost?
Pricing depends on the blank, guides, handle materials, decorative work, and intended use. Most Forever Rods builds fall between $175 and $400, with simpler builds starting around $125. Premium components and highly customized designs may cost more. You’ll receive a complete quote before any components are ordered.
